📘 LOTOTO Field Guide & P&ID Symbols

The 3-Step LOTOTO Sequence

  1. Lock & Tag (LOTO): Close and apply personal locks/tags to manual block valves & electrical MCC breakers. Watch isolated pipes automatically highlight in Yellow!
  2. Vent & Drain: Open vents, drains, or inter-valve bleeds on isolated segments to release trapped stored energy. The highlighter turns Emerald Green! (Never vent a live pressurized line!)
  3. Tryout (TO): Prove Zero Energy! Click local Start/Stop (HOA) switches to verify motors won't start, click Pressure Gauges (PI) at 0 PSI, and run Gas Monitors for confined space entry.

Watch Out for Industrial Traps!

  • Control Valves (FV / LV / PV): NOT valid positive isolation! Actuators can fail open and trim leaks. Always lock manual block valves.
  • Check Valves (CV): One-way flappers are NOT valid LOTO points—they cannot be locked and frequently leak backpressure.
  • Spectacle Blinds (SB): Required for hot work / line breaking / confined space—always isolate & depressurize before swinging a blind!
  • Keep-Live Units: Don't close a shared plant header if a standby pump or adjacent unit must stay online!
